P0455: Evaporative Emission System Leak Detected (Large Leak)

The system that traps gas fumes has a big leak. Check that the gas cap is on and clicked tight first.

How urgent is it?

Low Safe to drive. Get it looked at when it's convenient.

DIY or shop: Easy DIY

System: engine or transmission

Common causes of P0455

  • Missing or loose gas cap
  • Disconnected vapor hose
  • Stuck-open vent valve
  • Cracked charcoal canister

What to tell your mechanic

“I have a P0455. Can you smoke-test the EVAP system for a large leak, starting with the gas cap and vapor hoses?”

Read the code from your own car

Plug a USB OBD-II adapter into your car and use the free WOBD scanner in Chrome, Edge, or Opera to see which codes your car has stored. You can also look up another code.
