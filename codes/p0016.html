P0016: Crankshaft/Camshaft Position Correlation (Bank 1 Sensor A)

The crankshaft and camshaft aren't lined up the way the computer expects, often from a stretched or slipped timing chain or belt. On many engines a jumped chain can bend valves, so avoid driving until it's checked.

How urgent is it?

High Pull over when it's safe. Driving on this could damage the engine.

DIY or shop: Shop job

System: engine or transmission

Common causes of P0016

  • Stretched timing chain or slipped belt
  • Faulty variable valve timing solenoid
  • Bad cam or crank position sensor
  • Low or dirty engine oil

What to tell your mechanic

“I have a P0016 cam/crank correlation code. Can you check the timing chain and the VVT solenoid?”

Read the code from your own car

Plug a USB OBD-II adapter into your car and use the free WOBD scanner in Chrome, Edge, or Opera to see which codes your car has stored. You can also look up another code.
