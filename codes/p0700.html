P0700: Transmission Control System Malfunction

The transmission computer found a problem and saved its own code. A transmission scan will show the details.

How urgent is it?

Medium Drive gently and get it checked soon.

DIY or shop: Shop job

System: engine or transmission

Common causes of P0700

  • A fault stored in the transmission computer
  • Failing shift solenoid
  • Low or old transmission fluid

What to tell your mechanic

“I have a P0700. Can you read the transmission computer's codes?”

Read the code from your own car

Plug a USB OBD-II adapter into your car and use the free WOBD scanner in Chrome, Edge, or Opera to see which codes your car has stored. You can also look up another code.

Related codes
