P0122: Throttle/Pedal Position Sensor/Switch A Circuit Low

The throttle position sensor is reporting very low voltage. You may notice stumbling, poor response, or reduced power.

How urgent is it?

Medium Drive gently and get it checked soon.

DIY or shop: DIY if you're handy

System: engine or transmission

Common causes of P0122

  • Loose or damaged wiring
  • Failing throttle or pedal position sensor
  • Failed throttle body

What to tell your mechanic

“I have a P0122. Can you check the throttle position sensor circuit and wiring?”

Read the code from your own car

Plug a USB OBD-II adapter into your car and use the free WOBD scanner in Chrome, Edge, or Opera to see which codes your car has stored. You can also look up another code.
