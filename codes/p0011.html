P0011: Intake Camshaft Timing Over-Advanced (Bank 1)

The engine's valve timing is further ahead than the computer asked for. Low or dirty oil is often behind it.

How urgent is it?

Medium Drive gently and get it checked soon.

DIY or shop: DIY if you're handy

System: engine or transmission

Common causes of P0011

  • Low or dirty engine oil
  • Sticking variable valve timing solenoid
  • Stretched timing chain

What to tell your mechanic

“I have a P0011. Can you check the oil level and the cam timing solenoid first?”

Read the code from your own car

Plug a USB OBD-II adapter into your car and use the free WOBD scanner in Chrome, Edge, or Opera to see which codes your car has stored. You can also look up another code.
