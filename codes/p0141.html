P0141: O2 Sensor Heater Circuit (Bank 1 Sensor 2)

The built-in heater in the oxygen sensor after the catalytic converter isn't working.

How urgent is it?

Low Safe to drive. Get it looked at when it's convenient.

DIY or shop: Easy DIY

System: engine or transmission

Common causes of P0141

  • Failed heater inside the oxygen sensor
  • Blown fuse for the sensor heater
  • Damaged wiring or connector

What to tell your mechanic

“I have a P0141. Can you check the O2 heater fuse and wiring for the downstream sensor?”

Read the code from your own car

Plug a USB OBD-II adapter into your car and use the free WOBD scanner in Chrome, Edge, or Opera to see which codes your car has stored. You can also look up another code.
