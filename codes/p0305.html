P0305: Cylinder 5 Misfire Detected

Cylinder 5 isn't firing properly, so you may feel shaking or hesitation. If the check engine light is flashing, stop driving and have it towed. A steady light means drive gently and get it checked soon.

How urgent is it?

High Pull over when it's safe. Driving on this could damage the engine.

DIY or shop: DIY if you're handy

System: engine or transmission

Common causes of P0305

  • Worn spark plug in cylinder 5
  • Failing ignition coil or plug wire
  • Clogged or leaking fuel injector
  • Low compression

What to tell your mechanic

“I have a P0305. Can you swap the cylinder 5 coil with another cylinder to see if the misfire follows it?”

Read the code from your own car

Plug a USB OBD-II adapter into your car and use the free WOBD scanner in Chrome, Edge, or Opera to see which codes your car has stored. You can also look up another code.
