P0106: Manifold Absolute Pressure/Barometric Pressure Circuit Range/Performance

The manifold pressure sensor's reading doesn't match what the engine is doing. A vacuum leak or a failing sensor is common.

How urgent is it?

Medium Drive gently and get it checked soon.

DIY or shop: DIY if you're handy

System: engine or transmission

Common causes of P0106

  • Cracked or loose vacuum hose
  • Vacuum leak
  • Failing MAP sensor

What to tell your mechanic

“I have a P0106. Can you smoke-test for vacuum leaks and check the MAP sensor?”

Read the code from your own car

Plug a USB OBD-II adapter into your car and use the free WOBD scanner in Chrome, Edge, or Opera to see which codes your car has stored. You can also look up another code.
