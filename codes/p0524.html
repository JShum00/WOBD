P0524: Engine Oil Pressure Too Low

Your engine oil pressure is too low. Stop driving as soon as it's safe and check the oil level, because low oil pressure can ruin an engine fast.

How urgent is it?

High Pull over when it's safe. Driving on this could damage the engine.

DIY or shop: Shop job

System: engine or transmission

Common causes of P0524

  • Low engine oil level
  • Wrong or worn-out oil
  • Failing oil pump
  • Faulty oil pressure sensor

What to tell your mechanic

“I have a P0524. Can you check the actual oil pressure with a gauge?”

Read the code from your own car

Plug a USB OBD-II adapter into your car and use the free WOBD scanner in Chrome, Edge, or Opera to see which codes your car has stored. You can also look up another code.
